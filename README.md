## 📌 Overview 

This project analyzes **541K+ online retail transactions** to evaluate business performance through sales, customer, product, and geographic analysis. Using **Python** for data preparation, **SQL** for business analysis, and **Power BI** for visualization, the project transforms raw transactional data into actionable business insights.

---

## 🎯 Business Problem 

Retail businesses generate massive volumes of transactional data, making it difficult to identify revenue drivers and business opportunities.

This project addresses questions related to:
-  Sales Performance
-  Product Performance
-  Customer Analysis
-  Country-wise Revenue
-  Monthly Sales Trends
-  Revenue Growth Opportunities

---

## 📂 Dataset

- CSV file containing online retail transaction records.
- The dataset is sourced from Kaggle and is suitable for analyzing sales performance, customer behavior, product performance, and revenue trends.

## 🔍 Data Analysis using SQL

Performed business analysis in **SQL** to:

- Analyze revenue, orders, and product performance.
- Identify top-selling products and high-value customers.
- Evaluate country-wise revenue and monthly sales trends.
- Calculate average order value and order quantity.
- Generate insights to support business decision-making.

---

## 💡 Key Insights

- **£10.64M** total revenue from **19,960** orders.
- **3,922** unique products sold.
- **United Kingdom** generated the highest revenue.
- **November 2011** recorded peak sales.
- High-selling products were not always the highest revenue generators.
